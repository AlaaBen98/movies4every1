Synopsis

After a plane crash, former Special Forces soldier James Belmont and his retired military dog, Odin, are stranded in the Alaskan wilderness. Facing freezing temperatures, dangerous terrain and unpredictable wildlife, they must rely on their training and their bond to survive.

About the Movie

Heart of the Beast is a survival adventure about loyalty, endurance and the bond between a former soldier and his dog. Its story places the pair in a remote wilderness where every decision matters.
