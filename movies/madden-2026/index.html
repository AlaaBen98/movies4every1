Synopsis

Madden follows John Madden's remarkable journey, from his Super Bowl-winning partnership with Raiders owner Al Davis to becoming one of football's most recognizable broadcasters and helping create the Madden NFL video game franchise.

About the Movie

Inspired by the life of John Madden, the film explores his career as a coach, broadcaster and cultural figure, alongside his close partnership with Al Davis and his influence on the world of American football and sports gaming.
