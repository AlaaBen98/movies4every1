Synopsis

After her baby brother is abducted by a murder of crows, teenager Prue McKeel ventures into the Impassable Wilderness, an enchanted forest beyond Portland, Oregon. Joined by her classmate Curtis, she encounters talking animals, bandits and powerful figures as she fights to bring her brother home.

About the Movie

Wildwood is a stop-motion animated fantasy adventure from LAIKA, directed by Travis Knight and based on the novel by Colin Meloy, with illustrations by Carson Ellis.
